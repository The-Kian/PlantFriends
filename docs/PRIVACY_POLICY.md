# Plant Friends Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Plant Friends ("we", "the app") is made by Kian Popat. This policy explains what data the app collects and what we do with it.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your email address and display name. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address (or Apple's private relay address) and name that provider shares.
- **Your plants:** the plants you add and the details you enter, such as names, notes, room, watering schedule and watering history.
- **Your household:** every account belongs to a household, which starts as just you. Anyone you invite with a code joins it. Members of a household can see each other's **display name**, the plants in the household (shared plants, and each other's personal plants) and **watering activity**, such as who watered a plant and when. Invite codes expire after 7 days.
- **Notifications:** to send reminders we store your phone's **push notification token** and your **time zone**, plus your notification choices (watering reminders, housemate activity).
- **Crash and diagnostic data:** when the app crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects technical information such as device model, OS version, the error, and an account identifier. It does not include your email or plant notes.
- **Plants you add to the shared catalogue:** if you add a plant species that isn't in our catalogue yet, other users can see that species entry. It is not linked to your name.

Watering reminders and housemate notifications are sent from our server through Expo's push service, which receives your push token and the message text. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell your data.

## How we use it

To run your account and household, store and sync your plants, send watering reminders and housemate notifications (not during 21:00 to 08:00 in your time zone), and fix bugs.

## Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check and Crashlytics), and push messages are delivered through Expo's push service. Plant searches are sent through our server to the Trefle plant database. Only your search text is sent, never your account details.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in **Profile → Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account, email address, display name, push token, notes, custom plant names and your link to every plant.

**If you share a household:** the plants stay with the housemates who remain. Plants only you looked after become shared with them. Your display name is removed from the household's member list, but a plant may still say "watered by" your name until it is next watered. If you were the only member, the household and its plants are deleted.

We keep an **anonymous** copy of your plant care data: the plant species, room type, watering frequency and watering dates. It has no account ID, name, email or free text, so it cannot be linked back to you. We use it to improve plant care recommendations.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us at kian.popat@gmail.com.
