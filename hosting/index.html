Common questions

I'm not getting watering reminders. Check that notifications are allowed for Plant Friends in your phone's settings. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so they appear once a plant has a watering schedule.

How do I delete my account? In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account. This removes your account and personal data straight away. See the privacy policy for details.

I forgot my password. On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?" to get a reset email.
