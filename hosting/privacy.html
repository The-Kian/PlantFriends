Plant Friends Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Plant Friends ("we", "the app") is made by Kian Popat. This policy explains what data the app collects and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and display name. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address (or Apple's private relay address) and name that provider shares.
  • Your plants: the plants you add and the details you enter, such as names, notes, room, watering schedule and watering history.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: when the app crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects technical information such as device model, OS version, the error, and an account identifier. It does not include your email or plant notes.
  • Plants you add to the shared catalogue: if you add a plant species that isn't in our catalogue yet, other users can see that species entry. It is not linked to your name.

Watering reminders are scheduled on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

To run your account, store and sync your plants, send watering reminders, and fix bugs.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Crashlytics). Plant searches are sent through our server to the Trefle plant database. Only your search text is sent, never your account details.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, email address, display name, notes, custom plant names and your link to every plant.

We keep an anonymous copy of your plant care data: the plant species, room type, watering frequency and watering dates. It has no account ID, name, email or free text, so it cannot be linked back to you. We use it to improve plant care recommendations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us at kian.popat@gmail.com.

Children

PlantFriends is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU and UK).

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
